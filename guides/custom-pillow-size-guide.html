Custom Pillow Size Guide: Everything You Need to Know

Updated: January 12, 2026 | 12 min read | By Holiday Gift Guide Team

Choosing the right pillow size is crucial for both aesthetic appeal and functional comfort. Whether you're designing decorative throw pillows for your living room, creating personalized bedding accents, or crafting custom gifts, understanding pillow sizing ensures your projects turn out exactly as envisioned. This comprehensive guide covers everything from standard dimensions to insert selection and fabric considerations.

Understanding Standard Pillow Sizes

While custom dimensions are always an option, standard pillow sizes have evolved to meet common needs and preferences. Familiarizing yourself with these standards helps in making informed decisions and ensures compatibility with off-the-shelf inserts and cases.

Throw Pillow Sizes

Throw pillows are primarily decorative and come in several standard sizes:

  • 12x12 inches: Small accent pillows, perfect for chairs or as decorative accents on larger arrangements
  • 16x16 inches: The most common throw pillow size, versatile for sofas, chairs, and beds
  • 18x18 inches: Standard medium size, provides substantial presence without overwhelming furniture
  • 20x20 inches: Large statement pillows, ideal for sectionals or as focal points
  • 22x22 inches: Extra-large pillows, used sparingly for dramatic effect in spacious rooms

Bed Pillow Sizes

Bed pillows serve both functional and decorative purposes:

  • Standard (20x26 inches): The most common bed pillow size, fits standard pillowcases
  • Queen (20x30 inches): Slightly longer than standard, provides extra coverage for queen beds
  • King (20x36 inches): The longest standard size, ideal for king-sized beds
  • European (26x26 inches): Square decorative pillows often used behind standard sleeping pillows

Specialty Pillow Sizes

Custom projects often require non-standard dimensions:

  • Bolster pillows: Cylindrical pillows ranging from 6x12 inches to 9x24 inches
  • Neck roll pillows: Smaller cylindrical pillows (4x12 inches to 6x16 inches) for neck support
  • Floor pillows: Large squares (24x24 to 36x36 inches) for casual seating
  • Lumbar pillows: Rectangular (12x20 inches to 14x30 inches) for lower back support

Choosing the Right Size for Your Space

Selecting the appropriate pillow size depends on several factors beyond personal preference:

Furniture Scale

Consider the scale of your furniture when choosing pillow sizes:

  • Armchairs: 16x16 or 18x18 inches work well, one or two pillows maximum
  • Sofas: Mix of 18x18 and 20x20 inches, typically 3-5 pillows depending on sofa length
  • Sectionals: 20x20 and 22x22 inches, 5-7 pillows to fill the extended space
  • Beds: Layer European squares (26x26) behind standard sleeping pillows (20x26)

Room Proportions

The size of your room should influence pillow selection:

  • Small rooms: Opt for smaller pillows (16x16 inches) to avoid overwhelming the space
  • Medium rooms: Standard sizes (18x18 inches) provide balanced proportions
  • Large rooms: Mix sizes including larger pillows (20x20 inches) for visual weight

Design Purpose

Consider the pillow's role in your overall design:

  • Accent pillows: Smaller sizes (12x12 to 16x16 inches) for pops of color and pattern
  • Primary pillows: Standard sizes (18x18 inches) as main decorative elements
  • Statement pillows: Larger sizes (20x20 to 22x22 inches) for focal points

Pillow Insert Selection Guide

The insert significantly affects how your custom pillow looks and feels. Proper insert selection ensures professional results:

Sizing Your Insert

For the best appearance, choose an insert that's slightly larger than your cover:

  • 16x16 cover: Use 18x18 insert for full, plush appearance
  • 18x18 cover: Use 20x20 insert for optimal fullness
  • 20x20 cover: Use 22x22 insert for substantial, luxurious look

The extra size allows the insert to fill the corners completely, creating a smooth, professional appearance without visible gaps or sagging.

Insert Fill Materials

Different fill materials offer varying levels of support, durability, and maintenance requirements:

Polyester Fiberfill

  • Affordable and widely available
  • Lightweight and easy to maintain
  • Hypoallergenic options available
  • May compress over time requiring fluffing
  • Ideal for decorative pillows

Feather and Down

  • Luxurious, soft feel
  • Excellent shape retention
  • Natural insulation properties
  • Higher cost than synthetic options
  • Requires professional cleaning
  • Not suitable for allergy sufferers

Memory Foam

  • Excellent support and pressure relief
  • Maintains shape well over time
  • Ideal for lumbar and neck support pillows
  • Can retain heat
  • Higher cost

Alternative Materials

  • Buckwheat hulls: Natural, supportive, breathable
  • Kapok: Natural, silk-like feel, hypoallergenic
  • Microbeads: Conforming support, lightweight

Fabric Selection for Custom Pillows

The fabric you choose affects both the appearance and durability of your custom pillows:

Indoor Fabrics

Polyester

  • Excellent for custom printing with vibrant color reproduction
  • Durable and resistant to fading
  • Easy to clean – machine washable
  • Wide range of textures and finishes available
  • Affordable option for most projects

Cotton

  • Natural, breathable material
  • Soft feel and comfortable texture
  • Good for simple designs and text
  • May wrinkle more than synthetic options
  • Requires more careful maintenance

Linen

  • Premium natural material with distinctive texture
  • Excellent breathability
  • Natural wrinkle effect adds character
  • Higher cost
  • Requires professional cleaning

Outdoor Fabrics

Sunbrella and Solution-Dyed Acrylics

  • Designed to withstand UV exposure without fading
  • Water and mildew resistant
  • Easy to clean with mild soap and water
  • Durable construction for outdoor use
  • Higher initial cost but longer lifespan

Olefin

  • Synthetic fabric resistant to moisture and chemicals
  • Quick-drying and stain resistant
  • Affordable outdoor option
  • Less breathable than natural options

Design Considerations for Different Sizes

Your design approach should vary based on pillow size:

Small Pillows (12x12 to 16x16 inches)

  • Keep designs simple and uncluttered
  • Use larger elements and text for visibility
  • Avoid intricate details that may be lost at small scale
  • Consider how multiple small pillows will work together

Medium Pillows (18x18 inches)

  • Versatile size for most design approaches
  • Can accommodate moderate detail and text sizes
  • Works well for both photography and graphic designs
  • Ideal size for most custom printing projects

Large Pillows (20x20 inches and larger)

  • Can handle complex, detailed designs
  • Excellent for multi-photo collages
  • Consider how the design will appear when pillow is partially compressed
  • Ensure sufficient resolution for larger print area

Frequently Asked Questions

What size insert should I use for my pillow cover?

For a full, plush look, choose an insert that's 1-2 inches larger than your cover size. For example, use an 18x18 insert for a 16x16 cover, or a 20x20 insert for an 18x18 cover. This extra size allows the insert to fill the corners completely, creating a smooth, professional appearance without visible gaps or sagging. The slight overfill also helps the pillow maintain its shape and provides a more luxurious feel.

What are the standard throw pillow sizes?

Standard throw pillow sizes are 16x16 inches (small), 18x18 inches (medium), and 20x20 inches (large). These sizes work well for most furniture and design applications. The 18x18 inch size is particularly popular as it provides substantial presence without overwhelming furniture. Mixing sizes within a standard range creates visual interest while maintaining design cohesion.

What fabric is best for custom pillows?

Polyester and polyester-cotton blends are excellent for custom printing due to their durability, color reproduction, and ease of care. Cotton offers natural softness but may require more maintenance. For outdoor use, solution-dyed acrylics like Sunbrella provide UV resistance and weather durability. Consider your specific needs – printing quality, durability, care requirements, and intended use – when selecting fabric.

How many pillows should I put on my sofa?

The general rule is to use odd numbers for visual appeal. For a standard three-cushion sofa, three to five pillows work well. Place one pillow on each end and one in the center, or use asymmetrical arrangements. Avoid overcrowding as it can make the space feel cluttered. Consider the sofa's size and your room's overall design when determining the optimal number.

Can I mix different pillow sizes on the same furniture?

Absolutely! Mixing sizes creates visual interest and depth. A common approach is to use larger pillows (20x20 inches) at the ends of sofas with smaller pillows (16x16 or 18x18 inches) toward the center. This creates a pleasing graduated effect. Ensure the colors and patterns complement each other to maintain design cohesion despite the size variation.
