Ultimate Guide to Custom Blankets for Holiday Gifts

Updated: January 15, 2026 | 12 min read | By Holiday Gift Guide Team

Custom blankets have become one of the most thoughtful and personalized gift options for holiday seasons. Whether you're shopping for family members, friends, or colleagues, a personalized blanket combines practical warmth with sentimental value, making it a present that's both useful and meaningful. This comprehensive guide will help you navigate the world of custom blankets, from understanding different materials to design tips that ensure your gift becomes a treasured keepsake.

Why Custom Blankets Make Perfect Holiday Gifts

The appeal of custom blankets as holiday gifts lies in their unique combination of functionality and personalization. Unlike generic presents, a custom blanket shows that you've put thought into selecting something specifically for the recipient. These blankets serve multiple purposes – they provide warmth and comfort, decorate living spaces, and preserve precious memories through photos and designs.

Research shows that personalized gifts are perceived as more thoughtful and are kept longer than mass-produced items. A custom blanket featuring family photos, artwork, or meaningful designs becomes a conversation piece and a cherished possession that reminds the recipient of your thoughtfulness every time they use it. During the holiday season, when temperatures drop and people spend more time at home, a cozy, personalized blanket is particularly timely and appreciated.

Types of Custom Blankets Available

Understanding the different types of custom blankets available helps you choose the best option for your specific gift-giving needs. Each type has unique characteristics that make it suitable for different purposes and recipients.

Fleece Blankets

Fleece blankets are the most popular choice for custom gifts due to their affordability, softness, and excellent print quality. Made from synthetic polyester fibers, fleece blankets are lightweight yet warm, making them ideal for year-round use. The material accepts dye-sublimation printing exceptionally well, allowing for vibrant, full-color designs that cover the entire blanket surface.

Fleece blankets come in various weights, from lightweight throws perfect for summer evenings to heavier versions suitable for winter warmth. They're machine washable, quick-drying, and resistant to pilling, ensuring the gift maintains its appearance and functionality over time. The soft texture makes fleece particularly appealing for children and those with sensitive skin.

Woven Blankets

Woven blankets offer a more traditional, textured feel that many recipients appreciate. Instead of printing onto the surface, woven blankets incorporate the design into the fabric structure itself by weaving colored threads together. This creates a durable, heirloom-quality blanket with a distinctive look and feel.

The weaving process limits color options (typically 8-12 colors), so woven blankets work best with bold, simple designs rather than photographic images. The textured surface adds visual interest and makes these blankets particularly suitable for rustic or traditional decor styles. Woven blankets tend to be more expensive than fleece options but offer exceptional durability and a premium feel.

Weighted Blankets

Weighted blankets have gained tremendous popularity for their therapeutic benefits, making them excellent gifts for people who experience anxiety, sleep difficulties, or sensory processing issues. These blankets contain glass beads or plastic pellets distributed evenly throughout the fabric to provide gentle pressure that mimics the feeling of being hugged.

Custom weighted blankets can be personalized with photos or designs, combining therapeutic benefits with sentimental value. They're particularly thoughtful gifts for college students, new parents, or anyone dealing with stress. Weight options typically range from 5 to 25 pounds, with the general recommendation being to choose a weight that's about 10% of the recipient's body weight.

Photo Blankets

Photo blankets specifically focus on displaying images, making them perfect for preserving family memories, celebrating milestones, or honoring loved ones. These blankets can feature single large photos, collages of multiple images, or combinations of photos with text and decorative elements.

Photo blankets are available in both fleece and woven varieties, with fleece offering better color reproduction for detailed images and woven providing a more traditional aesthetic. These make excellent gifts for grandparents (featuring grandchildren photos), newlyweds (wedding photos), or as memorial tributes. The key to successful photo blankets is using high-resolution images and carefully planning the layout to ensure important elements aren't lost in seams or folds.

Choosing the Right Material

The material you select significantly affects the blanket's feel, durability, and suitability for different uses. Consider these factors when making your choice:

Polyester Fleece

Polyester fleece is the most common material for custom blankets due to its excellent printing characteristics and affordability. It's soft, lightweight, and provides good insulation without being heavy. Fleece is also hypoallergenic, making it suitable for people with allergies. The material is machine washable and dries quickly, though it can be prone to static electricity in dry conditions.

Cotton

Cotton blankets offer natural breathability and a traditional feel that many people prefer. They're softer than synthetic options and become more comfortable with each wash. However, cotton doesn't accept printing as vividly as polyester, and it's more prone to shrinking and wrinkling. Cotton blankets are ideal for recipients who prefer natural materials and are willing to accept some limitations in print quality.

Acrylic

Acrylic blankets provide wool-like warmth at a lower price point and are often used for woven blankets. They're lightweight, warm, and resistant to moths and mildew. Acrylic can sometimes feel less soft than other materials and may pill over time, but it offers good durability and color retention. These blankets work well for people who want warmth without weight.

Blends

Many custom blankets use material blends to combine the benefits of different fibers. Common blends include cotton-polyester (combining natural feel with printing performance) and acrylic-polyester (offering warmth with durability). Blends can provide an excellent balance of characteristics, though they may have specific care requirements depending on the fiber mix.

Size Guide for Custom Blankets

Selecting the right size ensures your gift is practical and well-suited to the recipient's needs:

Small Blankets (30x40 inches)

Small blankets are perfect for babies, young children, or as decorative throws. They're ideal for use in strollers, car seats, or as security blankets for toddlers. For adults, small sizes work well as lap blankets or for adding a decorative touch to chairs and small reading nooks.

Medium Blankets (50x60 inches)

Medium blankets are the most versatile size and work well for most adults. They're large enough to wrap around shoulders while sitting on a couch or chair, yet not so large that they're cumbersome. This size is ideal for everyday use in living rooms, home offices, or as an extra layer on beds.

Large Blankets (60x80 inches)

Large blankets provide full coverage for adults and work well as bed blankets or for sharing. They're substantial enough to cover a twin-sized bed completely and can accommodate two people sitting together. Large blankets make impressive gifts but require more storage space and may be too warm for some users in mild weather.

Extra-Large Blankets (70x90 inches and larger)

Extra-large blankets are designed for use on full or queen-sized beds or for those who want maximum coverage. These substantial blankets make dramatic gifts but consider the recipient's bed size and storage capabilities before choosing this size. They're particularly well-suited for tall individuals or couples who share blankets.

Design Tips for Custom Blankets

Creating an effective design ensures your custom blanket looks professional and effectively conveys your intended message or sentiment:

Image Quality and Resolution

The quality of your source images directly impacts the final result. Use high-resolution photos (at least 300 DPI at the final print size) to ensure sharp, clear printing. Avoid using low-resolution images from social media, as these often appear pixelated when enlarged to blanket size. If you must use lower-quality images, choose simpler designs and avoid close-up portraits that might show quality issues.

Layout and Composition

Plan your layout carefully, considering how the blanket will be used and viewed. Keep important design elements away from the edges (at least 1-2 inches from borders) to account for seam allowances and potential trimming. Consider how the design will appear when the blanket is folded – some designs work better when they create pleasing patterns when folded.

Color Considerations

Choose colors that complement the recipient's home decor and personal preferences. If you're unsure, neutral backgrounds with colorful accents tend to work well. Remember that colors may appear slightly different in print than on your monitor, so avoid relying on exact color matching for critical design elements. Test print your design at a smaller scale if color accuracy is crucial.

Text and Typography

If including text, choose fonts that are legible at the blanket's viewing distance. Avoid fonts smaller than 24-30 points, as smaller text may be difficult to read from a typical viewing distance. Consider how text will appear when the blanket is draped or folded – important messages should be placed where they'll remain visible in typical use scenarios.

Personalization Options

Beyond photos and graphics, consider adding personal touches like names, dates, quotes, or meaningful locations. These elements add sentimental value and make the blanket uniquely meaningful to the recipient. However, balance personalization with overall design – too many elements can make the blanket feel cluttered and reduce visual impact.

Special Holiday Design Ideas

Seasonal and holiday-specific designs can make your gift particularly timely and meaningful:

Family Photo Collages

Create collages featuring family photos from throughout the year, particularly great for grandparents or family members who live far away. Arrange photos chronologically to show family growth and changes over time, or group them by themes like vacations, celebrations, or everyday moments. Leave space between photos for captions or dates to add context.

Holiday Tradition Themes

Design blankets that celebrate family holiday traditions – perhaps featuring photos from annual Christmas gatherings, Thanksgiving celebrations, or other yearly events. These blankets become increasingly meaningful as years pass and document family traditions and growth. Consider including the family name or establishment date for a generational keepsake.

Memorial and Tribute Designs

For recipients who have lost loved ones, memorial blankets featuring photos and meaningful quotes can provide comfort during holiday seasons when grief is often heightened. These sensitive gifts should be designed with care, focusing on positive memories and celebrating the person's life rather than emphasizing loss.

Pet-Themed Blankets

For pet lovers, blankets featuring beloved animals make excellent gifts. Use high-quality pet photos and consider adding the pet's name, birth date, or adoption date. These blankets are particularly appreciated during holiday seasons when pets are often included in family celebrations and gift-giving.

Ordering Timeline and Production

Proper planning ensures your custom blanket arrives in time for holiday gift-giving:

Production Time

Standard production time for custom blankets typically ranges from 2-5 business days, though this can vary significantly during peak holiday seasons. Some companies offer rush production options for additional cost. Complex designs or special requests may require longer production times. Always confirm production timelines when placing your order, especially for time-sensitive holiday gifts.

Shipping Considerations

Standard shipping typically takes 3-7 business days within the continental United States, while international shipping can take 10-14 days or longer. During holiday seasons, shipping delays are common, so building in buffer time is essential. Consider shipping directly to the recipient if you won't see them before the holiday, but confirm they'll be available to receive the package.

Quality Review

Most companies provide digital proofs of your design before production. Review these carefully, checking image quality, text accuracy, and overall layout. Request changes if needed – it's much easier to fix issues at the proof stage than to receive a blanket that doesn't meet your expectations. Some companies offer physical samples for an additional cost, which can be worthwhile for large or important orders.

Care and Maintenance Instructions

Include care instructions with your gift to ensure the blanket maintains its appearance and function:

Washing Guidelines

Most custom blankets can be machine washed in cold water with similar colors. Use mild detergent and avoid bleach, which can damage both the fabric and printed design. For fleece blankets, avoid fabric softeners, which can reduce the fabric's moisture-wicking properties and affect print quality.

Drying Recommendations

Tumble dry on low heat or air dry to preserve both the fabric and printed design. High heat can cause shrinkage and may affect print quality. Remove blankets promptly from the dryer to minimize wrinkles. For best results, shake blankets before drying to redistribute any filling and maintain even loft.

Storage Tips

When not in use, store blankets in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, which can cause fading over time. Avoid storing in plastic bags, which can trap moisture and lead to mildew. Instead, use breathable cotton bags or simply fold and place in a linen closet. For long-term storage, consider adding cedar blocks or lavender sachets to deter moths.

Budget Considerations

Custom blankets are available at various price points, allowing you to find options within your budget:

Price Factors

Blanket prices vary based on size, material, design complexity, and quantity. Fleece blankets are typically the most affordable option, while woven and weighted blankets command higher prices. Larger sizes naturally cost more, and some companies charge design fees for complex layouts or extensive image editing.

Quantity Discounts

If you're ordering multiple blankets (perhaps for family members or a group gift), many companies offer quantity discounts. These can significantly reduce the per-unit cost, making custom blankets more affordable for larger gift lists. Some companies also offer referral discounts or seasonal promotions that can help with budget management.

Value Considerations

While custom blankets may cost more than mass-produced alternatives, consider their value as meaningful, personalized gifts. The emotional impact and long-term usefulness often justify the higher price point. For particularly important recipients, investing in higher-quality materials or larger sizes can demonstrate the significance of your relationship.

Conclusion

Custom blankets offer a unique combination of practicality and personalization that makes them ideal holiday gifts. By understanding the different types available, choosing appropriate materials and sizes, and putting thought into the design, you can create a gift that provides warmth and comfort while serving as a lasting reminder of your thoughtfulness. Whether you're celebrating family bonds, honoring traditions, or simply showing someone you care, a custom blanket is a gift that will be treasured for years to come.

Start planning your custom blanket gift early to allow adequate production and shipping time, especially during busy holiday seasons. With proper planning and attention to detail, your personalized blanket will become a highlight of holiday celebrations and a cherished possession that brings comfort and joy long after the holiday season has passed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What type of custom blanket is best for holiday gifts?

Fleece blankets are the most popular choice for holiday gifts due to their softness, affordability, and excellent print quality. They're available in various sizes and work well for both photo and graphic designs. However, woven blankets offer a more traditional, premium feel, while weighted blankets provide therapeutic benefits. Consider the recipient's preferences and needs when selecting the type.

How long does it take to create and ship custom blankets?

Production time typically ranges from 2-5 business days, with shipping adding another 3-7 business days depending on your location. During peak holiday seasons (November-December), production times may extend to 7-10 business days, and shipping delays are common. To ensure timely delivery for holiday gifts, order at least 3-4 weeks in advance and consider choosing expedited shipping options.

What's the best way to design a custom blanket as a gift?

Start with high-quality photos at 300 DPI resolution, consider the recipient's preferences in colors and themes, and design with the blanket's orientation in mind. Leave adequate margin space (1-2 inches from edges) and test your design at actual size before ordering. Include personal touches like names, dates, or meaningful quotes, but avoid overcrowding the design. Consider how the blanket will be used and folded when planning your layout.

Can I wash custom blankets in the washing machine?

Yes, most custom blankets are machine washable. Wash in cold water with similar colors using mild detergent. Avoid bleach and fabric softeners, which can damage the fabric and affect print quality. Tumble dry on low heat or air dry for best results. Always check the specific care instructions provided with your blanket, as recommendations may vary based on material and printing method.

What size blanket should I choose for an adult gift?

For most adults, a medium blanket (50x60 inches) is the most versatile choice – large enough to wrap around shoulders while sitting but not cumbersome. Large blankets (60x80 inches) work well for taller individuals or those who prefer more coverage. Consider the recipient's typical use – if they'll use it primarily on a couch, medium is usually ideal; if for bed use, large may be better.
