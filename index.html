Frequently Asked Questions

What is print-on-demand (POD)?

Print-on-demand is a business model where products are created only when a customer places an order. This means no inventory costs and the ability to offer unlimited customization options for blankets, pillows, and t-shirts. Each item is made specifically for you, reducing waste and ensuring perfect quality.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the continental United States. Express shipping options are available for 2-3 business day delivery. International shipping typically takes 10-14 business days depending on the destination country and customs processing.

Can I return custom products?

Due to the custom nature of our products, returns are only accepted for manufacturing defects or damage during shipping. Please contact our customer service within 30 days of delivery if you experience any issues with your order. We'll work with you to make it right.

What file formats do you accept for custom designs?

We accept high-resolution JPG, PNG, and PDF files. For best results, we recommend vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) or raster images with at least 300 DPI resolution. Our design team can also help convert lower-quality images into print-ready files.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes! We offer competitive bulk discounts for orders of 10 or more items. The discount increases with quantity - 10-24 items get 10% off, 25-49 items get 15% off, and 50+ items receive 20% off. Contact our sales team for custom quotes on large orders.
