Shipping Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

Processing Time

Custom products are made to order. Standard processing time is 2-5 business days before shipping. During peak holiday seasons, processing times may be extended.

Shipping Options

Standard Shipping (5-7 Business Days)

Free on orders over $50. $5.99 on orders under $50.

Express Shipping (2-3 Business Days)

$12.99 flat rate.

International Shipping (10-14 Business Days)

$19.99 flat rate. Additional customs fees may apply depending on destination country.

Shipping Destinations

We currently ship to:

  • United States (all 50 states)
  • Canada
  • Select international destinations

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can track your order using the provided tracking number.

Shipping Address

Please ensure your shipping address is correct at time of order. We cannot modify shipping addresses once orders have been processed.

Undeliverable Packages

If a package is returned as undeliverable, we will contact you to arrange reshipment. Additional shipping fees may apply for reshipment.

Shipping Questions

For shipping-related inquiries, contact shipping@holidaygiftguide.shop
