Refund Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

Custom Products

Due to the personalized nature of our custom products, we generally do not accept returns or exchanges. Each item is made specifically for you based on your design specifications.

Defective or Damaged Items

If your order arrives with manufacturing defects or damage during shipping, we will gladly replace it at no additional cost. Please contact us within 30 days of delivery with:

  • Your order number
  • Photos showing the defect or damage
  • Description of the issue

Wrong Items

If you receive an item different from what you ordered, we will correct the mistake by sending the correct item or issuing a full refund, depending on your preference.

Refund Process

For approved refunds, we process refunds to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. The timing of your refund may vary depending on your payment provider.

Order Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placement. After this time, production begins and cancellations may not be possible. Contact us immediately if you need to cancel an order.

Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refund:

  • Custom products with no manufacturing defects
  • Items damaged due to customer misuse
  • Items returned after 30 days

How to Request a Refund

Contact our customer service team at refunds@holidaygiftguide.shop with your order details and issue description. We will respond within 24-48 business hours.

Customer Satisfaction

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you experience any issues with your order, please contact us so we can make it right.
