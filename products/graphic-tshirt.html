Frequently Asked Questions

What printing method do you use for t-shirts?

We primarily use DTG (Direct-to-Garment) printing, which allows for detailed, full-color designs with excellent color accuracy. For larger orders (50+ pieces), we also offer screen printing, which can be more cost-effective for simple designs with fewer colors. Both methods produce high-quality, durable prints.

How should I wash my custom t-shirt?

To maintain the quality of your custom t-shirt, machine wash it inside out in cold water with similar colors. Use mild detergent and avoid bleach or fabric softeners. Tumble dry on low heat or hang dry to prevent shrinking. Do not iron directly on the printed design - if ironing is necessary, iron inside out or use a pressing cloth.

Do you offer plus sizes?

Yes, we believe everyone deserves great custom apparel. Our t-shirts are available in sizes XS to 3XL, with detailed measurements provided in our size chart. The relaxed fit ensures comfort across all sizes, and our premium cotton has minimal shrinkage for consistent sizing wash after wash.

Can I print on both front and back?

Absolutely! We offer printing on both the front and back of t-shirts. You can choose to have the same design on both sides, different designs, or print on just one side. Our design tool makes it easy to visualize and customize both sides before placing your order.

What file formats do you accept for t-shirt designs?

We accept high-resolution PNG, JPG, and PDF files. For best results, we recommend vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) or high-resolution raster images (300 DPI or higher). Our design team can help convert lower-quality images or assist with creating your design if needed.
