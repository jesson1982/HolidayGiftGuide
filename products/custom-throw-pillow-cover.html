Frequently Asked Questions

Does this come with a pillow insert?

No, this product is for the pillow cover only. However, we offer premium pillow inserts that are sold separately and designed to fit perfectly with our covers. The inserts are made from high-quality polyester fiberfill that provides excellent support and maintains its shape over time.

What size insert should I buy?

For a full, plush look, we recommend choosing an insert that's 1-2 inches larger than your cover size. For example, use an 18x18 insert for a 16x16 cover, or a 20x20 insert for an 18x18 cover. This extra size ensures the pillow looks full and maintains its shape without straining the seams.

Can I use this outdoors?

While our pillow covers are durable and fade-resistant, they are designed primarily for indoor use. They can withstand occasional outdoor use in covered areas, but we recommend bringing them inside during inclement weather or prolonged sun exposure to maintain their appearance and extend their lifespan.

How do I clean the pillow cover?

Our pillow covers are machine washable for easy care. Remove the insert, then wash the cover in cold water with similar colors using mild detergent. Avoid bleach and fabric softeners. Tumble dry on low heat or air dry. The hidden zipper ensures the cover stays secure during washing.

Can I order matching sets?

Absolutely! We offer bulk discounts for orders of multiple pillow covers, making it affordable to create coordinating sets. Whether you want matching covers for your couch or a variety of complementary designs, our bulk pricing makes it easy to decorate your entire space with custom pillow covers.
