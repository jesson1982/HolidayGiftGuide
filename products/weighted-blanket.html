Frequently Asked Questions

What weight should I choose for my weighted blanket?

The general rule is to choose a weight that's about 10% of your body weight, plus or minus a pound. For example, if you weigh 150lbs, a 15lb blanket would be ideal. If you're new to weighted blankets, we recommend starting with a lighter weight to see how your body responds. Children and elderly individuals should consult with a healthcare provider before using weighted blankets.

Is the weighted blanket machine washable?

Yes, our weighted blankets are designed for easy care. Machine wash in cold water on a gentle cycle using mild detergent. We recommend using a laundry bag to protect the blanket and prevent the beads from shifting. Tumble dry on low heat or air dry. Avoid bleach and fabric softeners, which can damage the fabric and affect the weight distribution.

What are the benefits of a weighted blanket?

Weighted blankets provide deep pressure stimulation, which has been shown to reduce anxiety, improve sleep quality, and promote relaxation. The gentle pressure mimics the feeling of being hugged, which can increase serotonin and melatonin levels while decreasing cortisol. Many people report falling asleep faster and staying asleep longer when using a weighted blanket regularly.

How is the custom design applied to the weighted blanket?

We use a special printing process designed for weighted blankets that ensures your custom design adheres perfectly to the fabric without affecting the weight distribution or therapeutic benefits. The printing is durable and can withstand regular washing without fading or peeling, maintaining both the appearance and functionality of your blanket.

Can children use weighted blankets?

Weighted blankets can be beneficial for children, but it's important to choose the appropriate weight and consult with a pediatrician first. For children, we recommend selecting a weight that's 10% of their body weight plus 1-2 pounds. Never use adult-sized weighted blankets for children, and always supervise use to ensure safety.
