Frequently Asked Questions

What material is the fleece blanket made of?

Our premium fleece blankets are made from 100% high-quality polar fleece that's soft, warm, and lightweight. The material is breathable and hypoallergenic, making it perfect for sensitive skin. The fleece is also durable and resistant to pilling, ensuring your blanket stays soft and looking great wash after wash.

How do I care for my custom fleece blanket?

Machine wash your fleece blanket in cold water with similar colors. Use mild detergent and avoid bleach or fabric softeners, which can affect the print quality. Tumble dry on low heat or air dry for best results. Do not iron or dry clean. Following these care instructions will help maintain the vibrant colors and soft texture of your custom blanket.

What size options are available?

We offer three convenient sizes to suit different needs: Small (30x40 inches) perfect for babies, pets, or as a decorative throw; Medium (50x60 inches) ideal for adult use on couches and chairs; Large (60x80 inches) great for beds or wrapping around multiple people. Each size maintains the same premium quality and printing capabilities.

How is the design applied to the blanket?

We use advanced dye-sublimation printing technology that infuses the ink directly into the fabric fibers. This creates a permanent, vibrant print that won't crack, peel, or fade over time. The printing covers the entire blanket surface edge-to-edge, allowing for complete design freedom and stunning visual results.

Can I wash the blanket after printing?

Absolutely! Our printing process creates permanent designs that can withstand regular washing. Follow the care instructions: machine wash cold with mild detergent, avoid bleach, and tumble dry low. Your custom design will remain vibrant and intact even after multiple washes, making this blanket both beautiful and practical.
