Frequently Asked Questions

Is the pillow cover removable?

Yes, our photo pillows feature a hidden zipper closure, making the cover removable and machine washable for easy care and maintenance. Simply remove the insert, wash the cover in cold water with mild detergent, and air dry or tumble dry on low heat.

What quality of photo should I upload?

For best results, upload high-resolution photos (at least 300 DPI) with good lighting and clear focus. We recommend JPEG or PNG formats for optimal quality. If you have a lower-quality image you'd like to use, our design team can enhance it to ensure the best possible print result.

Does the pillow come with an insert?

Yes, each photo pillow comes with a premium polyester fiberfill insert that provides excellent support and maintains its shape over time. The insert is designed to fit perfectly within the cover and can be removed for washing or replaced if needed.

Can I print on both sides of the pillow?

Absolutely! We offer double-sided printing options, allowing you to feature different photos or designs on each side of the pillow. This is perfect for showcasing multiple memories or creating a reversible look that changes your room's aesthetic.

How durable is the print quality?

Our advanced printing process creates permanent, vibrant images that resist fading even with regular use and washing. The ink is infused into the fabric fibers rather than sitting on top, ensuring your photo remains crisp and colorful for years to come.
